How to Tell Time in English ⏰

Master reading analog clocks, expressions (o'clock, half past, a quarter to/past), and distinguish between A.M. and P.M.

Try Live Clock Sandbox

1. Analog vs Digital Time

Analog Clock: Has 12 numbers and 2 main hands: Short hand (Hour) and Long hand (Minute).

📟 Digital: 08:15 = "Eight fifteen"

🕒 Analog: 08:15 = "A quarter past eight"

2. A.M. vs P.M.

In English, we use 12-hour format with A.M. and P.M.

a.m. (Ante Meridiem)
12:00 Midnight to 11:59 Morning (e.g. 7:00 a.m. Breakfast)
p.m. (Post Meridiem)
12:00 Noon to 11:59 Night (e.g. 8:00 p.m. Sleep)

3. Key Terms

  • O'clock When minute hand is at 12 (00 min)
  • A quarter 15 minutes (1/4 of an hour)
  • Half past 30 minutes (1/2 hour)
  • Past vs To Minutes 1-30: PAST | Minutes 31-59: TO
